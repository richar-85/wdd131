About This Site

Building friendships and fitness through weekend soccer.

Our Mission

The Weekend Soccer Pickup Game website was created to bring members of the local community together through the love of soccer. It provides a simple and reliable way to share game information, including schedules, locations, and participation details. Our mission is to promote physical activity, teamwork, and social interaction by making it easy for players of all skill levels to find and join weekend soccer games.

Who Can Join?

Anyone interested in recreational soccer is welcome. Whether you are a beginner learning the game or an experienced player looking for weekend competition, you'll find a place here.

Why Join Us?

  • Stay active and healthy
  • Meet new people in the community
  • Improve soccer skills
  • Enjoy a fun and supportive environment
