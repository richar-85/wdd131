About This Site

The Weekend Soccer Pickup Game website was created to bring members of the local community together through the love of soccer. It provides a simple and reliable way to share game information, including schedules, locations, and participation details.

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote physical activity, teamwork, and social interaction by making it easy for players of all skill levels to find and join weekend soccer games.
